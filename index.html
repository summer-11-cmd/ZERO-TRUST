Risk Mitigation & Enterprise Safeguards

Addressing your CISO, OT Director, and Procurement team's key deployment concerns.

No Rip-and-Replace Guarantee

Legacy PLCs, unmanaged OT controllers, and sensitive medical hardware are protected via ZGuard Transparent Edge Gateways without requiring firmware modifications or hardware replacement.

  • Supports legacy serial, Modbus, BACnet, Profinet
  • Zero downtime proxy deployment

Observe-Before-Enforce Engine

All security rules run in Monitor & Shadow Mode for a mandatory 14-day baseline period. Traffic is analyzed and mapped without dropping a single valid industrial frame.

  • Simulated enforcement impact reports
  • Automated policy refinement wizard

Phased Site-by-Site Rollback

Rollout is executed in micro-zones (single assembly line, laboratory, or building wing). If any anomaly occurs, one-click fallback instantly restores legacy bypass state.

  • Sub-second fail-open hardware bypass
  • Blast radius strictly isolated to single zone
